About The Training

What Is This Training All About?

The Digital Resource Acquisition and Monetization Academy is a practical training programme designed to introduce participants to the world of digital learning resources and online digital product entrepreneurship.

Participants will learn how to discover and access valuable online courses across different niches, organize digital learning materials, establish a digital product seller account on Selar and explore ways of selling digital products to customers locally and internationally.

The training is structured around practical demonstrations and guided activities so participants can understand how the process works and apply what they learn.
